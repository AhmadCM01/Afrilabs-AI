Hub: YANDA -Centre Of Innovation And Entrepreneurship. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/yanda-centre-of-innovation-and-entrepreneurship/. Description: Yanda Centre for Innovation & Entrepreneurship empowers entrepreneurs through inclusive support, skill development, and business growth services. It offers tailored training in science, technology, and engineering for blind and deaf youth, and connects them with SMEs for meaningful employment. The centre also supports innovation through ideation, business strategy, design thinking, funding access, IP registration, and prototyping.
