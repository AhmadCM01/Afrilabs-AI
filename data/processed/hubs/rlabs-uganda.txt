Hub: RLabs Uganda. Country: Uganda. Region: Eastern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/rlabs-uganda/. Description: RLabs (Reconstructed Living Lab) is a hub dedicated to empowering communities through innovation, technology, and entrepreneurship. Since inception, RLabs has provided training, mentorship, and support to youth, women, and marginalized groups across more than 23 countries. Through its labs and incubators, RLabs fosters grassroots solutions that drive social impact, economic inclusion, and sustainable development.
