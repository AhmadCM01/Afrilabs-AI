Hub: I’M IN STARTUP HUB. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/im-in-startup-hub/. Description: The I’M IN Startup Hub is a high-growth community of Startups focused on the African technology sector, providing opportunities for entrepreneurs with technical skills who have limited access to resources.
