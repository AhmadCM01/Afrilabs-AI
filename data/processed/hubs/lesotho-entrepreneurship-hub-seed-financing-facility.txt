Hub: Lesotho Entrepreneurship Hub & Seed Financing Facility. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/lesotho-entrepreneurship-hub-seed-financing-facility/. Description: The Lesotho Entrepreneurship Hub & Seed Financing Facility is a government initiative under the Ministry of Trade, Industry, Business Development, and Tourism. The hub aims to unite all ecosystem players by empowering Enterprise Support Organizations (ESOs) to incubate businesses across various sectors, with a focus on digital entrepreneurship. The initiative targets innovative businesses, strengthening them to play a significant role in the economy.
