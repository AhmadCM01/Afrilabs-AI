Hub: JHUB Africa. Country: Kenya. Region: Eastern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/jhub-africa/. Description: JHUB Africa is an incubator that develops climate-smart agricultural solutions and advances digital trade. It cultivates a dynamic innovation ecosystem by fostering creativity, collaboration, and cross-disciplinary innovation. JHUB Africa provides comprehensive support, mentorship, and resources to digital entrepreneurs, startups, and tech experts within its ecosystem. Additionally, it facilitates networking opportunities to connect innovators with industry leaders and potential collaborators.
