Hub: Boris Bison Youth Empowerment Business Incubator (BBYEBI). Country: Cameroon. Region: Central Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/boris-bison-youth-empowerment-business-incubator-bbyebi/. Description: The Boris Bison Youth Empowerment Business Incubator (BBYEBI) is a strong advocate for the Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and is dedicated to mentoring startups and young people to cultivate an environmentally conscious and profitable economy. By leveraging its extensive network of qualified stakeholders and facilitators, BBYEBI empowers young Africans with entrepreneurial skills through a comprehensive program that includes coaching, training, mentoring, and monitoring. This program aims to identify sustainable and profitable pathways for scalable growth across Africa.
