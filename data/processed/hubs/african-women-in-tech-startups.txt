Hub: African women in tech startups. Country: Cameroon. Region: Central Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/african-women-in-tech-startups/. Description: African Women In Tech Startup is a tech hub that aims to prepare women to seize empowerment opportunities offered by technology through entrepreneurship. It also supports women entrepreneurs in leveraging digital technology for growth, regardless of their sector. Through their AfricanWITS MeetUp initiative, they aim to create a platform dedicated to showcasing women entrepreneurs and leaders, providing a space where they can connect with strategic partners, access funding, and receive training and guidance to strengthen their leadership.
