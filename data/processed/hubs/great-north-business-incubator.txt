Hub: Great North Business Incubator. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/great-north-business-incubator/. Description: The Great North Business Incubator (GNBI) is a multi-sector incubator dedicated to empowering entrepreneurs to transform local challenges into sustainable businesses. GNBI offers expert mentorship, training, and scalable support across sectors such as youth development, education, health, mining, environment, tourism, and enterprise. Its mission is to build resilient ventures that drive inclusive economic growth across Sub-Saharan Africa.
