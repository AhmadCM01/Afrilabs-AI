Hub: CETESA- Centro de Estudios para la Transformación Económica y Social de África. Country: Equatorial Guinea. Region: Central Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/cetesa-centro-de-estudios-para-la-transformacion-economica-y-social-de-africa/. Description: CETESA operate as a Pan-African incubation center founded in Senegal with a regional representation in the Equatorial Guinea, is dedicated to supporting young entrepreneurs. It operates across key sectors such as technology, health, education, food production, and trade. Its mission is to equip young project leaders with appropriate technical tools, provide high-quality training in business management, and offer structured support throughout the incubation and development phases of their entrepreneurial ventures. CETESA also delivers personalized guidance and advisory services to strengthen the sustainability and growth of SMEs.
