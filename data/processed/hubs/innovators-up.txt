Hub: Innovators Up. Country: Ghana. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/innovators-up/. Description: Innovators Up is a vibrant hub for entrepreneurship, dedicated to unlocking the potential of local innovators. By offering resources, training, mentorship, and networking opportunities, it equips them to thrive in today’s competitive landscape. Innovators Up claims to be a movement that champions talent, fosters collaboration, and empowers innovators to push boundaries, challenge the status quo, and create new pathways for economic and social transformation.
