Hub: Collective Lab (Affiliate to Nile University of Nigeria). Country: Nigeria. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/collective-lab-affiliate-to-nile-university-of-nigeria/. Description: The Collective Lab (also known as the Entrepreneurship & Innovation Hub) is a unit in the Student Services department with the mission of developing an innovative entrepreneurial spirit among Nile University’s students. The Collective Lab provides a dedicated space for students to meet, learn, connect, create, share, and inspire, as well as the requisite guidance and support to start their own businesses.
