Hub: TiC Foundation. Country: Cameroon. Region: Central Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/tic-foundation/. Description: TiC is a dynamic enterprise support organization dedicated to cultivating the success of startup founders, equipping them to develop sustainable, prosperous, and scalable ventures. At the forefront of shaping Africa’s technological landscape, TiC fosters innovation, entrepreneurship, and technological advancement through a multifaceted approach encompassing networking, training, mentorship, and accelerator initiatives. With a particular focus on nurturing the entrepreneurial spirit in Cameroon, TiC empowers young founders to harness technology for pioneering solutions to local challenges. By championing this mission, TiC is fueling the emergence of a new generation of techpreneurs, poised to lead Africa’s tech revolution.
