Hub: INOVTECH FOUNDATION. Country: Ghana. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/inovtech-foundation/. Description: INOVTECH delivers high-quality STEM education, industry-relevant skills, and hands-on training. The INOVTECH STEM Center focuses primarily on robotics education, covering the full spectrum of STEAM subjects (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics). INOVTECH’s mission is to raise awareness, spark interest, and build capacity among Africans in STEAM fields, encouraging greater participation across the continent.
