AfriLabs Innovation Hub Directory by Country and Region

AfriLabs is Africa's largest network of innovation hubs. Below is a directory of AfriLabs member hubs organized by country. AfriLabs member innovation hubs are located in the following countries: Algeria, Burkina Faso, Burundi, Cameroon, Congo Democratic Republic, Egypt, Equatorial Guinea, Ghana, Guinea, Ivory Coast, Kenya, Lesotho, Malawi, Nigeria, Sierra Leone, South Africa, Tanzania, Tunisia, Uganda, and Zimbabwe. These hubs span Northern Africa, Western Africa, Central Africa, Eastern Africa, and Southern Africa.
