Hub: Trailblazer Digital Hub. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/trailblazer-digital-hub/. Description: Trailblazer Digital Hub is a Level One BBBEE-rated non-profit organization founded in 2020, dedicated to empowering individuals and businesses in South Africa to thrive in the digital economy. We provide accessible ICT solution bundles—including software development, data science training, and specialized digital skills programs—designed especially for youth and the unemployed. Our mission is to drive innovation, digital inclusion, and entrepreneurship by equipping people with the tools, skills, and support they need to succeed in today’s tech-driven world.
