Hub: CIATECH Africa. Country: Nigeria. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/ciatech-africa/. Description: CIA Tech – Center for Innovation and Advanced Technology is a tech-driven social enterprise dedicated to advancing innovation across industries. The hub provides a collaborative space for research, development, and knowledge exchange, bringing together researchers, startups, local communities, and industry leaders. With a strong focus on climate innovation, CIA Tech supports startups through targeted research, tailored incubation programs, and strategic partnerships. It also conducts needs assessments to identify the financial and operational gaps faced by tech-enabled and creative sector SMEs and startups, helping them scale sustainably.
