Hub: Wislaw Education and Support Fund. Country: Ghana. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/wislaw-education-and-support-fund/. Description: Wislaw Education and Support Fund is a development-focused social enterprise dedicated to empowering entrepreneurs as agents of change in their communities. The organization equips them with the skills and funding needed to leverage unique opportunities in Ghana and across Africa. Wislaw achieves this through capacity building, financing, networking opportunities, market access, learning and development, coaching, mentoring, internships, and by providing reliable and insightful information.
