Hub: Mektoub. Country: Burkina Faso. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/mektoub/. Description: Mektoub is a pioneering hub committed to fostering entrepreneurship and innovation in Burkina Faso by empowering young, aspiring entrepreneurs, supporting development agencies, and engaging vulnerable communities through targeted, impactful programs tailored to local needs. Mektoub promotes the concepts of innovation and entrepreneurship by producing and sharing animated videos in local languages.
