For Hubs For Corporates What Are Innovation Hubs? According to the AfriLabs and Briter Bridges ecosystem report, an innovation hub is a community and learning centre that fosters innovative ideas and market disruption. These hubs support creative problem-solving through on-ground support and consultancy throughout the start-up lifecycle. They are centres that offer support to African entrepreneurs, innovators, developers, and youths. By providing physical co-working spaces, dedicated office spaces, inclusive training, and business, legal, and financial support, they help in fostering successful entrepreneurs who will create jobs and develop innovative African-centered solutions.
