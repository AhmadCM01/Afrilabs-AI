Hub: KIVUTECH. Country: Congo Democratic Republic Of The. Region: Central Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/kivutech/. Description: KivuTech is a business incubator focused on promoting and supporting entrepreneurs in the Democratic Republic of Congo. The organization also works to improve education, and socio-economic reintegration, promote gender equality, and reduce unemployment. KivuTech offers a wide range of services and programs to help entrepreneurs develop their business ideas and bring them to fruition. These include training and mentoring programs, financing opportunities, collaborative workspaces, and networking events.
