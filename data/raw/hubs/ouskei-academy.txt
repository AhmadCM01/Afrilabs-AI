Hub: OUSKEI ACADEMY. Country: Guinea. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/ouskei-academy/. Description: Ouskei Academy is a FabLab and training center dedicated to teaching Science, Technology, Engineering, and Mathematics (STEM). It aspires to become a leading hub for STEM and robotics education in Guinea and beyond. Their vision is to support a community of passionate youth in improving STEM education and robotics in Guinea. An effort that leads to the creation of startups, organizations, and the bridging of skills gaps in the job market. Through its commitment to teaching, research, and innovation, Ouskei Academy’s exceptional community actively pursues its mission of serving both nationally and globally.
