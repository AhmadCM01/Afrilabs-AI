Hub: African Center for Enterprise & Capacity Development (Center-ECD). Country: Nigeria. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/african-center-for-enterprise-capacity-development-center-ecd/. Description: The African Center for Enterprise & Capacity Development (Center-ECD) is a hub for entrepreneurship, social enterprise, and human capital development. Operating as an innovation hub and co-working space, Center-ECD fosters an ecosystem where human capital is optimized for social impact and global good through excellence in education and entrepreneurship. Center-ECD offers exclusive, customized programs tailored to the specific needs of entrepreneurs and businesses in leadership and management. Through these initiatives, it systematically enhances the practice of entrepreneurial leadership and management across the African continent.
