Hub: M.D.E “MAISON DE L’ENTREPRENEUR”. Country: Burundi. Region: Eastern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/m-d-e-maison-de-lentrepreneur/. Description: La Maison Des Entrepreneurs aims to be a business incubator. It works primarily with young entrepreneurs, both rural and urban, supporting them from the ideation stage through to the development of their businesses. This is achieved through training, coaching, advocacy, support, and assistance in securing funding with the ultimate goal of enabling young people in Burundi to create their own jobs and generate employment for others.
