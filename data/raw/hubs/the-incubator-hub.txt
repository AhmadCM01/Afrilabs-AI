Hub: The Incubator Hub. Country: Nigeria. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/the-incubator-hub/. Description: The Incubator Hubs aim to promote digital education in line with Goal 4 of the UN Sustainable Development Goals, ensuring inclusive and equitable quality education and fostering lifelong learning opportunities for all. They strive to provide an optimal collaborative environment within their community. Their mission also focuses on developing the next generation of innovators and entrepreneurs who will drive national development through technological and educational advancements key tools for addressing social and economic challenges in line with the UN Sustainable Development Goals and other future international collaborative agendas.
