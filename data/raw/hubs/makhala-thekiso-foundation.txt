Hub: Makhala Thekiso Foundation. Country: Lesotho. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/makhala-thekiso-foundation/. Description: The Makhala Thekiso Foundation is an entrepreneurship skills development center aimed at supporting individuals who face challenges in traditional education. They provide tech literacy and empower young people in Mokhotlong, helping them increase their participation in the economy and promote sustainable livelihoods.
