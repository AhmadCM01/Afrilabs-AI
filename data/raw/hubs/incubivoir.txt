Hub: Incub’Ivoir. Country: Ivory Coast Cote Divoire. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/incubivoir/. Description: Incub’Ivoir is an incubator for innovative projects specializing in the agricultural sector. Focused on structuring agricultural value chains, the incubator has expertise in supporting agricultural entrepreneurs to generate and capture the added value present in this sector. The incubator’s mission is to inspire, train, structure, and support potential entrepreneurs to become agents of change within their communities.
