Hub: SHAMBA BOX. Country: Tanzania. Region: Eastern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/eight2five-innovation-hub/. Description: Shamba Box is an incubation space that focuses on transforming the food system and agricultural value chain in Tanzania. They support farmers and agripreneurs by offering a range of programs, including an acceleration program, research and data collection, project development, planning and management, and food and agricultural consultancy services.
