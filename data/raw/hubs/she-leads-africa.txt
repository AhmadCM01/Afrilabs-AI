Hub: She Leads Africa. Country: Nigeria. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/she-leads-africa/. Description: She Leads Africa is an organization dedicated to empowering women through access to a supportive community and supporting them to achieve their entrepreneurial and career journeys. The organization hosts a range of digital and in-person platforms designed to support women in building successful careers, growing thriving businesses, achieving financial independence.
