Hub: Empire Partner Foundation. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/empire-partner-foundation/. Description: The Empire Partner Foundation Tech Hub is a non-profit organization established to empower youth in building a better South Africa and the African continent through technology. Its mission is to leverage technology to create sustainable impact across South Africa and the continent. The hub engages youth and entrepreneurs to address 10 key sector challenges: Water, Energy, Transportation, Rural Development, Education, Unemployment, Healthcare, Housing, Agriculture, and Safety & Security.
