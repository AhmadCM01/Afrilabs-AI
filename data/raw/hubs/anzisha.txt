Hub: Anzisha. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/anzisha/. Description: Anzisha is a incubator dedicated to driving economic transformation across Africa by fostering youth-led enterprises. The organization supports young entrepreneurs through targeted funding, bespoke business development training, structured leadership growth opportunities, and access to a supportive network of peers and experienced mentors. By equipping Africa’s youth to become resilient job creators, Anzisha contributes meaningfully to the advancement of sustainable, inclusive economic growth on the continent.
