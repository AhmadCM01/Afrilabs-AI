Hub: Graca Machel Trust – Women Creating Wealth. Country: South Africa. Region: Southern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/graca-machel-trust-women-creating-wealth/. Description: Graca Machel Trust – Women Creating Wealth is a women’s development support platform to empower 10,000 women to generate a billion dollars and create 200,000 jobs in five years. This is achieved by strengthening women entrepreneurs’ confidence in wealth creation, facilitating access to markets and finance, and providing an ongoing support platform.
