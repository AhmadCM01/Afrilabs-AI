Hub: Ogandaze Africa Foundation. Country: Uganda. Region: Eastern Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/ogandaze-africa-foundation/. Description: Ogandaze Africa Foundation (OAF) is a women-led organization empowering youth, women, and marginalized groups including persons with disabilities through entrepreneurship and inclusive economic opportunities. By promoting micro-enterprises, business savings groups, agribusiness, and financial literacy, OAF is tackling poverty and unemployment while equipping communities with the skills and tools to thrive.
