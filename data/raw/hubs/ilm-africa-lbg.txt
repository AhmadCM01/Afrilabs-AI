Hub: ILM Africa LBG. Country: Ghana. Region: Western Africa. Website: https://www.afrilabs.com/hub/ilm-africa-lbg/. Description: ILM Africa Hub is a dynamic organization dedicated to helping businesses grow, innovate, and make a positive impact. Their services include business consulting, research and development in AI and renewable energy, and advisory support for corporate social responsibility (CSR). ILM Africa also run programs like the Upanuzi Accelerator, which helps businesses scale quickly, and the Nikela Fund, which connects investors with high-growth African businesses.
